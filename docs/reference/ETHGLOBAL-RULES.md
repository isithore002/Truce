## 3. ETHGlobal general rules (Mumbai)

### Takeaway
In-person hackathon (IRL), Thu Nov 5 to Sat Nov 7; submission deadline Sat Nov 7 09:00 AM IST (late submissions not accepted). Teams up to 5 (solo allowed). Up to 3 partner prizes per project (same-sponsor tracks count as one). Must be physically present and checked in. Classic ("From Scratch") vs Continuity tracks. AI allowed with disclosure. Demo video 2-4 minutes optional but strongly encouraged. Finalists: top 10, 7-minute session.

### Cited Findings
- Dates/format: ETHGlobal Mumbai, Nov 5-7 (Thu-Sat), Mumbai, India, "IRL Hackathon"; Pragma Mumbai conference Nov 6 (Fri). — [ETHGlobal events](https://ethglobal.com/events) (re-verified). Conflict: an iq.wiki page citing an ETHGlobal table lists the hackathon as Nov 6-8 and Pragma Nov 5; the ETHGlobal events page is primary and says Nov 5-7. — [iq.wiki](https://www.iq.wiki/en/wiki/ethglobal) (secondary, likely stale)
- Venue: official ETHGlobal pages fetched do not name it. Jio World Convention Centre, BKC, from a secondary calendar; Devcon 8 is in Mumbai Nov 3-6. — [CriptoNoticias](https://www.criptonoticias.com/calendario-eventos/ethglobal-mumbai-2026/) (secondary, unverified)
- Hacking hours/opening ceremony/start time: NOT found. The /info/start page defers to the schedule tab at /events/mumbai#schedule, which could not be fetched. — [Start page](https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai/info/start)
- IRL: quote from the rules page: "You must officially check-in and be physically present at the venue to participate if said event is happening in-person." Stake only returned if minimum requirements met (check-in and on site). Wristband must be worn at all times. — [ETHGlobal Rules](https://ethglobal.com/rules). So remote-only participation is not possible for an in-person event (inference from that rule; the /info/details page itself states no IRL rule).
- Team size: up to 5; solo allowed; each member must be accepted and stake ETH individually; stake returned after submitting, usually within four weeks. — [Start page](https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai/info/start)
- Under-18 needs guardian consent form. — [ETHGlobal Rules](https://ethglobal.com/rules)
- Classic "From Scratch": "all work on your project must begin after the hackathon officially starts"; projects built earlier may participate but won't qualify for partner prizes or Finalist. Continuity (Extend Open Source / Ship a Feature): may build on an existing codebase, must document pre-existing work and show new functionality; partner eligibility varies. — [Details](https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai/info/details)
- Version control required; "large single commits or missing histories may be disqualified." Open-source libraries and starter kits allowed if disclosed; include GitHub repo, Figma, or equivalent proving work done during the hackathon. — [Details](https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai/info/details); [Rules](https://ethglobal.com/rules)
- AI: "generally permitted", must document where/how used; AI should assist, not create the whole project (entirely-AI projects may be ineligible for partner prizes/Finalist); spec-driven workflows allowed but all spec files, prompts and planning artifacts must be in the repo. — [Details](https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai/info/details)
- Submission: via Hacker Dashboard; form asks title, description, repo link. Two options: (1) Finalist + Partner prizes (requires presenting to judges at the Finalist session), (2) Partner prizes only (explain how you used each partner's tools + feedback; no judge presentation). Partner booth presentations optional. Up to 3 partner prizes. — [Details](https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai/info/details)
- Finalist session: 7 minutes (4 demo + 3 Q&A). Finalist judging criteria (5): Technicality, Originality, Practicality, Usability (UI/UX/DX), WOW factor. The /info/start page instead lists "creativity, functionality, technical difficulty, and the impact of the solution" (two ETHGlobal pages differ; treat all as relevant). — [Details](https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai/info/details); [Start page](https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai/info/start)
- Partner judging: /info/start says participants present at partner booths; /info/details says booth presentations are optional. — [Start page](https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai/info/start); [Details](https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai/info/details)
- Demo video: optional but strongly encouraged; 2-4 minutes (outside range rejected at upload); min 720p; no mobile-phone recording; no speed-up; no TTS/AI voiceover; no music with text replacing narration; max 4 bullets per slide; backstory under 20 s. — [Details](https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai/info/details)
- Finalist prizes (top 10): per person solo 250 USDC; 2 people 250 + 250 travel; 3-5 people 1,000 + 500 travel; Finalist Pack: $10,000 AWS credits, $828 off ETHGlobal Plus, hoodie. — [Prizes](https://ethglobal.com/events/mumbai/prizes) (re-verified)
- Consequence for misrepresentation: project may be disqualified, prizes revoked, team banned. — [Rules](https://ethglobal.com/rules)
- Sponsor video/doc add-ons: World integration debrief; Arkiv /arkiv/*.md; Uniswap FEEDBACK.md; Whitechain feedback file; Hedera demo <= 5 min; 0G demo < 3 min.

### Inferences
- Real build window is about 2 days (Thu start, Sat 9 AM deadline); sponsor-mandated written deliverables (Arkiv five .md files, World debrief) should be written continuously.
- Pre-existing ExamShield code would make the project ineligible for Classic partner prizes; to use Continuity tracks the team must have enrolled in Continuity. Whether an existing team is enrolled is outside what I could verify.
- The one-vs-many video limits matter: the ETHGlobal video must be 2-4 min while Hedera tolerates 5; plan one 3-4 minute video that shows the Arkiv read, World denied path, and Swarm step with timestamps for swarm.md.

### Gaps

- Whether registration/acceptance is still possible (secondary source says closed Nov 3, unverified).
- Sanctions/KYC and prize payout terms were not stated on the rules page fetched; prior notes say prizes are paid by ETHGlobal in USDC (from the Whitechain page).
